1. Overview

Gnosis is a liturgical calendar application for Orthodox and Catholic Christians developed by Giangerostudio. We believe your faith and devotional practice are deeply personal. Core devotional records stay on your device. Optional online features and advertising use third-party services as described below; advertising may involve device identifiers and ad interaction data.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Application accesses, how it is handled, and your rights under global privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data Collection & Storage Architecture

2.1 Core Calendar & Liturgical Features

For the core calendar features, Gnosis does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. There is no account creation, no login requirement, and no profile tracking.

All user preferences and personal liturgical records are stored strictly locally on your device, including:

  • Tradition and calendar preferences (Eastern Orthodox Old/New Calendar, Roman Catholic).
  • Language, region, and fast notification preferences.
  • Custom personal name day reminders and saved family commemorations.
  • Scripture bookmarks, reading history, and offline cached calendar datasets.
  • Locally tracked Virtual Candle timers.

These locally stored devotional records are not supplied to the advertising SDK by Gnosis. Operating system-level device backups may include local app data. Advertising and other online services handle separate data as described below.

2.2 "Word of God AI" & Google AI Search Mode

If you voluntarily choose to use the interactive "Word of God AI" feature:

  • Query Transmission: The devotional or biblical search queries you enter are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS to Google search services ("Google AI Search Mode") to retrieve relevant public Scripture passages and contextual answers.
  • Zero Account Association: We do not attach your user identity, personal name, or device identifiers to these queries. Queries are processed anonymously to retrieve relevant answers.
  • Privacy Precaution: Please do not type sensitive personal information (such as real names, private life confessions, passwords, or financial data) into the search prompt.

2.3 Virtual Candles & In-App Billing Data

When you purchase or light a Virtual Candle in Gnosis:

  • Payments are processed exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing. We never view, collect, or store your credit card or financial credentials.
  • Google Play provides the Application with an anonymous cryptographic purchase token to verify the delivery of your digital candle. This verification is managed through official Google Play APIs.

2.4 Advertising in Version 7.1.0 and Later

The upcoming Android version 7.1.0 includes Google AdMob for one banner below the homepage news. Earlier versions do not contain this advertising integration. Development builds use test ads; live advertising is enabled separately.

Gnosis applies Google's most restrictive CHILD age treatment to every ad request, regardless of the user's age. We do not ask for or store an age or date of birth for advertising. This treatment disables personalized ads, remarketing, third-party ad-vendor requests and transmission of the Android advertising ID. The app also removes advertising-ID and Android AdServices/Topics permissions from its manifest. Restricted ads may still involve processing of IP address (including approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention; this is not a zero-data service.

Gnosis does not send your saved prayer reminders, bookmarks, family names or calendar-tradition preferences as advertising targeting parameters.

We also set the separate under-age-of-consent signal in Google's User Messaging Platform. Google suppresses its European consent request for that signal; we do not use suppression to request personalized ads. Requests still wait until the platform permits them. If Google requires a privacy-options entry, it appears in Settings → Advertising privacy. Reading and news remain available when advertising is unavailable.

See How Google uses information for advertising and Google's SDK data disclosures. Google's published age-treatment restrictions describe the protections applied to these requests.

3. Device Permissions & Purposes

Gnosis uses device permissions for reminders, online content and supported app features. Versions with AdMob also include SDK permissions for network status and advertising identifiers:

Permission Purpose
Internet (android.permission.INTERNET) To fetch public liturgical content, handle user-initiated Google AI Search queries, and load advertising/privacy messages in versions with advertising enabled.
Post Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS) To deliver local alerts for saints' days, fasting alerts, and name day commemorations.
Schedule Exact Alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) To trigger feast and prayer notifications at the precise local time selected by the user.
Receive Boot Completed (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) To restore your scheduled local feast notifications after you restart your mobile device.
Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE) To enable haptic vibration when a scheduled feast reminder alerts.
Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID)Explicitly removed from Gnosis's manifest in the restricted advertising configuration. Android advertising-ID transmission is also disabled by the SDK's CHILD age treatment.

4. Third-Party Services & Disclosures

Gnosis maintains a strictly privacy-focused stack. Specifically:

  • Google AdMob and UMP: Versions 7.1.0 and later include Google's advertising SDK and privacy messaging, as described in section 2.4. All ad requests use the restricted child-age treatment described above. Other SDK data may still be processed by Google for restricted ad delivery and related purposes.
  • Google AI Search Services: When actively using the "Word of God AI", queries are routed via Google search services to retrieve biblical insights. These interactions are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Used for secure in-app purchase verification of virtual candles.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Gnosis is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. A general-audience ad content rating is used to restrict inappropriate creatives; it is not age verification. If you believe a child's personal information has been processed inappropriately through the app, please contact us using the details below.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & Global Privacy)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy regulations, you have rights regarding your personal data. Because Gnosis stores your settings and data locally on your device:

  • Access & Portability: All your data is directly accessible within the Application interface on your device.
  • Deletion / Right to be Forgotten: You can completely erase all data, saved name days, and bookmarks at any moment simply by clearing the Application's cache/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling Gnosis.

For information processed by Google advertising services, use the Google privacy controls linked above. Clearing Gnosis's local storage does not itself delete data already held by Google. Contact us with privacy requests or concerns.

Visual Library and anonymous polls

Added 13 November 2026. The Visual Library downloads public catalog text and artwork from GitHub. Loading an Instagram player connects to Instagram; external Instagram links also use that service. Those providers process network requests under their own policies.

Voting does not require an account, name, email address or demographic information. The app stores a random voting credential privately on the device. Our Cloudflare-hosted voting service stores a hash of that credential, the poll choice and a technical update sequence to prevent accidental duplicate counting and allow answer changes. Individual choices and credential hashes are not published in the public catalog or displayed to other participants. Only aggregate counts and percentages are shown after voting.

Short-lived, hashed network-rate-limit buckets protect the service against excessive requests. Application code does not log voting credentials or vote request bodies. Infrastructure providers necessarily process network information to deliver the service. Clearing app storage creates a new anonymous identity and does not automatically remove earlier votes. We cannot identify an anonymous participant by name. Poll data is retained to support the permanent archive and can be withdrawn or removed through service administration.

Daily library reminders are optional and scheduled on your device. The service does not send a personal religious profile to Instagram, GitHub or advertising systems. AI artwork and generated reflections are identified in the app; Scripture text is supplied by the selected existing Bible edition.
