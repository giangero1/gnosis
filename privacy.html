1. Overview

Gnosis is a liturgical calendar application for Orthodox and Catholic Christians developed by Giangerostudio. We believe your faith and devotional practice are deeply personal. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy with a privacy-by-design architecture: we do not track you, sell your personal information, or operate remote surveillance databases.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Application accesses, how it is handled, and your rights under global privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data Collection & Storage Architecture

2.1 Core Calendar & Liturgical Features

For the core calendar features, Gnosis does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. There is no account creation, no login requirement, and no profile tracking.

All user preferences and personal liturgical records are stored strictly locally on your device, including:

  • Tradition and calendar preferences (Eastern Orthodox Old/New Calendar, Roman Catholic).
  • Language, region, and fast notification preferences.
  • Custom personal name day reminders and saved family commemorations.
  • Scripture bookmarks, reading history, and offline cached calendar datasets.
  • Locally tracked Virtual Candle timers.

This data never leaves your device unless you initiate an operating system-level device backup (e.g. Android Auto Backup or Google Drive device backup).

2.2 "Word of God AI" & Google AI Search Mode

If you voluntarily choose to use the interactive "Word of God AI" feature:

  • Query Transmission: The devotional or biblical search queries you enter are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS to Google search services ("Google AI Search Mode") to retrieve relevant public Scripture passages and contextual answers.
  • Zero Account Association: We do not attach your user identity, personal name, or device identifiers to these queries. Queries are processed anonymously to retrieve relevant answers.
  • Privacy Precaution: Please do not type sensitive personal information (such as real names, private life confessions, passwords, or financial data) into the search prompt.

2.3 Virtual Candles & In-App Billing Data

When you purchase or light a Virtual Candle in Gnosis:

  • Payments are processed exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing. We never view, collect, or store your credit card or financial credentials.
  • Google Play provides the Application with an anonymous cryptographic purchase token to verify the delivery of your digital candle. This verification is managed through official Google Play APIs.

3. Device Permissions & Purposes

Gnosis requests only the minimum device permissions strictly required to deliver calendar reminders and fetch public liturgical data:

Permission Purpose
Internet (android.permission.INTERNET) To fetch public liturgical calendar feeds and submit queries to Google AI Search Mode when the user actively opens the search feature.
Post Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS) To deliver local alerts for saints' days, fasting alerts, and name day commemorations.
Schedule Exact Alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) To trigger feast and prayer notifications at the precise local time selected by the user.
Receive Boot Completed (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) To restore your scheduled local feast notifications after you restart your mobile device.
Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE) To enable haptic vibration when a scheduled feast reminder alerts.

4. Third-Party Services & Disclosures

Gnosis maintains a strictly privacy-focused stack. Specifically:

  • NO Advertising Networks: We do not include AdMob, Unity Ads, or any third-party behavioral advertising SDKs.
  • NO Cross-App Tracking: We do not track users across other apps or websites, and we do not use advertising IDs.
  • Google AI Search Services: When actively using the "Word of God AI", queries are routed via Google search services to retrieve biblical insights. These interactions are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services: Used for secure in-app purchase verification of virtual candles.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Gnosis is designed to be family-safe and suitable for Christians of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because the Application does not collect personal identity data, compliance is built into the design.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & Global Privacy)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy regulations, you have rights regarding your personal data. Because Gnosis stores your settings and data locally on your device:

  • Access & Portability: All your data is directly accessible within the Application interface on your device.
  • Deletion / Right to be Forgotten: You can completely erase all data, saved name days, and bookmarks at any moment simply by clearing the Application's cache/storage in your device settings or by uninstalling Gnosis.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological improvements or regulatory updates. Any changes will be published immediately on this webpage with a revised "Last Updated" date.
