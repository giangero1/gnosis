3. Description of Services & Devotional Content

Gnosis is an interactive Christian liturgical calendar companion designed for Orthodox and Catholic Christians. The core features of the App are provided completely free of charge and include:

  • Eastern Orthodox (Old Calendar / Julian and New Calendar / Revised Julian) liturgical feast days, fasts, and saint commemorations.
  • Roman Catholic General Roman and National liturgical calendars, solemnities, and memorials.
  • Daily Scripture readings and the Synaxarion (Lives of the Saints).
  • Personalized name day reminders, local device feast notifications, and offline calendar access.
  • Scripture exploration and devotional inquiries via the "Word of God AI" feature.
Devotional & Spiritual Disclaimer: All liturgical information, feast dates, fasting guidelines, and saint commemorations are compiled in good faith for personal spiritual edification and devotional reference only. They do not constitute official ecclesiastical decrees, canonical judgments, or sacramental authority. Users should consult their local parish priest, bishop, or spiritual father for authoritative pastoral guidance and parish-specific rubrics.
